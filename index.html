Why we're making this

The money talk starts earlier than you think.

Most parents now start talking to their kids about spending and saving before age four — and nine in ten want their children taught more about money than they pick up at home. But almost everything out there is built for teenagers with debit cards.

Little kids don't need a card. They need to understand that a coin saved becomes something bigger, that a need comes before a want, and that sharing feels good. So we put it in songs — because that's how three-year-olds learn everything else.
